    Having Microphone Problems?

    1. Make sure your microphone is connected.
    2. Check that your browser has microphone permission.
    3. Check your operating system's microphone settings (Windows Sound Settings or macOS System Preferences > Sound).
    4. Make sure the correct microphone is selected.
    5. Try speaking closer to the microphone.
    6. Try another browser if the microphone still doesn't work.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I test my microphone online?

    Click "Test My Microphone" at the top of this page, allow your browser to access your microphone, and speak. You'll instantly see a real-time waveform, volume meter, and signal status. No downloads, no sign-up — everything runs directly in your browser using the Web Audio API.

    Why is my microphone not working?

    The most common reasons are:

    • Microphone not connected — Make sure your microphone is plugged in or paired via Bluetooth.
    • Browser permission denied — Click the lock icon in your browser's address bar and allow microphone access for this site.
    • Wrong device selected — Your computer may default to a different audio input. Check your operating system's sound settings.
    • Another app is using the mic — Close other apps like Zoom, Teams, or Discord that may be holding exclusive access.
    • Hardware mute switch — Some headsets and laptops have a physical mute button. Make sure it's off.
    Is this microphone test free?

    Yes, completely free. Mic Testing App is a free online tool with no downloads, no accounts, and no hidden costs. It works entirely in your browser.

    Is my audio recorded or uploaded?

    No. All audio processing happens locally in your browser using the Web Audio API. No audio data is ever uploaded to a server, stored, or transmitted. When you stop the test, the microphone stream is immediately released. Your privacy is guaranteed by the architecture of the tool itself.

    What browsers support online microphone testing?

    Mic Testing App works with all modern browsers:

    • Google Chrome (desktop & Android)
    • Mozilla Firefox (desktop & Android)
    • Microsoft Edge (desktop)
    • Apple Safari (14.1+ on macOS & iOS)

    A secure context (HTTPS or localhost) is required for microphone access.

    How do I fix a quiet microphone?
    • Speak closer to the microphone (within 15–30 cm / 6–12 inches).
    • Increase the microphone input level in your OS sound settings.
    • Make sure the correct microphone device is selected.
    • Disable excessive noise suppression or noise cancellation software.
    • Check if your microphone has a hardware gain dial and turn it up.
    How do I allow microphone permission in my browser?
    • Chrome / Edge: Click the lock (or tune) icon in the address bar → Site settings → Set Microphone to "Allow."
    • Firefox: Click the lock icon → Permissions → Check "Use the Microphone."
    • Safari: Go to Safari → Settings → Websites → Microphone → Set this site to "Allow."

    If you previously blocked permission, you may need to reset it and reload the page.

    Can I test my microphone on my phone or tablet?

    Yes. Mic Testing App is fully responsive and works on mobile devices. Supported mobile browsers include Chrome for Android, Safari for iOS (14.1+), and Firefox for Android. Simply open the site, tap "Test My Microphone", and allow permission when prompted.
